## Demo billing

The public pricing page and workspace use the same Starter, Growth and Scale tiers. Switching plans in the current demo updates the workspace tier; it does not charge a card or enforce rep/lead quotas. Real subscriptions, quota enforcement and billing notifications require a production billing integration.
